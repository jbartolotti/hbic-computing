External User Access Request
============================

Use this page if you are:

* Collaborating from another institution
* A visiting researcher
* A non-KUMC student
* Any other user who does not already have a university account

Before requesting access, please gather:

* Your name
* Your email address
* Your institution
* Your PI or laboratory affiliation
* The study or studies you will be working on
* The associated IRB number(s)

Copy the template below into an email and send it to James.

::

   Subject: New External User Access Request

   Name:

   Email:

   Institution:

   PI/Lab:

   Study Name(s):

   IRB Number(s):

   Additional Information:

What happens next?
------------------

You will be contacted with any additional requirements and instructions needed to access research resources.
